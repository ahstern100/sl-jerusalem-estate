Frequently Asked Questions

▼
1. What happens if there’s an emergency while I’m overseas?
We provide immediate emergency response dispatch. For Premium and Gold clients we are on-site during critical events and coordinate plumbers, electricians, or security as needed. You receive real-time updates via WhatsApp.
2. How do you handle Arnona (property tax) and utility bills?
We manage Arnona, water, electricity, and gas bills on your behalf. Bills are reviewed, paid when due (from an authorized account or with your approval), and you receive clear monthly summaries so there are never surprises.
3. Can you prepare the house for our arrival before Chagim or summer?
Yes. Premium and Gold packages include full Owner Arrival Prep: deep cleaning, HVAC testing and activation, fridge stocking with basics, fresh linens, and any special requests so the home is ready the moment you walk in.
4. Do you supervise contractors and repairs?
Absolutely. We source reliable, vetted local contractors, obtain quotes, supervise all work on-site, and only approve completion once the standard meets our (and your) expectations. You stay informed without having to manage from abroad.
5. How often will I receive updates about my property?
Basic Care includes a monthly photo/video status report. Premium and Gold clients receive more frequent updates, especially around maintenance visits, weather events, or any incidents. All communication is via WhatsApp for speed and convenience.
6. Is the service suitable for completely empty / unoccupied homes?
Yes. Basic Care is specifically designed for unoccupied properties. Regular inspections, ventilation, and bill management keep the home healthy and prevent the problems that arise from long vacancy.
7. What about insurance claims if something is damaged?
Gold Concierge includes full insurance-claim support and major renovation oversight. We document damage thoroughly, coordinate with your insurer, and manage the repair process end-to-end.
8. Can you help with short-term rentals or Airbnb if we decide to rent?
While our core focus is private estate care for owners, we can advise and coordinate trusted local partners for short-term rental management if you choose that route. Discuss your goals with Aharon for tailored recommendations.
9. How do you handle HVAC and air-conditioning systems?
We perform regular checks, seasonal start-up and shut-down, filter changes, and coordinate professional servicing. Before your arrival we ensure the system is clean, working, and set to a comfortable temperature.
10. What if I need keys or access arranged for family or guests?
We securely manage keys and can provide controlled access to approved family members or guests according to your written instructions. For Gold clients this is handled by your dedicated estate manager.
11. Do you offer services for vehicles left in Israel?
Yes. Gold Concierge includes vehicle maintenance checks, battery care, and coordination of servicing so your car is ready when you need it.
12. How quickly can you respond to a water leak or electrical issue?
Emergency dispatch is activated immediately upon notification. For Premium and Gold packages we aim for same-day on-site presence whenever possible, depending on the nature of the issue and location in Jerusalem.
13. Are your services available for apartments as well as houses and villas?
Yes. We manage apartments, townhouses, and full villas across Jerusalem. Package recommendations are based on property size, value, and your level of involvement rather than property type alone.
14. Can I change packages or pause service if my plans change?
Yes. Packages are flexible. Contact Aharon to upgrade, downgrade, or adjust the level of service according to your travel schedule or changing needs.
15. How do you ensure the home stays well-ventilated and free of mold?
Regular inspections include opening windows for ventilation (weather permitting), checking for moisture, and addressing any early signs of mold or dampness before they become serious problems.
16. What documentation or authorizations do you need from me?
Typically a simple management agreement, copies of relevant bills, and written authorization for payments and contractor access. We keep the process straightforward and transparent.
17. Do you provide housekeeping or deep-cleaning services?
Deep cleaning and ongoing housekeeping are included in Premium (arrival prep) and fully covered under Gold Concierge. We work with trusted, professional cleaners who know high-end properties.
18. Can you help with major renovations or upgrades?
Gold Concierge includes major renovation oversight—from contractor selection and permitting guidance to daily supervision and quality control—so you can upgrade your property without being on site.
19. Is there a minimum contract period?
We generally work on a month-to-month or annual basis depending on your preference. Discuss the best arrangement for your situation with Aharon; flexibility is important to us.
20. How do I get started?
Simply message Aharon on WhatsApp (+972-50-9499715) or use the contact form above. Tell us about your property and needs, and we’ll recommend the right package and next steps within a short time.
